GOATs

People whose work defines the standard of interface craft today. Follow them, read their blogs, recreate what they make.

Emil KowalskiDesign engineer at Linear, ex-Vercel. Author of Sonner, Vaul and the animations.dev course. Josh PuckettEx-Dropbox and Wealthfront. Author of Interface Craft and DialKit. "Uncommon care". Rauno FreibergDesign engineer at Vercel. Maintains the Craft catalog and ui.land. Paco CourseyDesign engineer at Linear. Author of cmdk and the "command menu" aesthetic. Dan HollickAuthor of Making Software, the illustrated manual on how screens, color, fonts and rasterization work. Josh W. ComeauReference for deep CSS: shadows, layout, animation. Author of CSS for JavaScript Developers. Mariana CastilhoDesign engineer, ex-Vercel. Author of Devouring Details, on interaction design. Adam ArgyleChrome DevRel. Explains new CSS APIs (OKLCH, scroll-driven animations, text-wrap) before everyone else. Jhey TompkinsCreative CSS demos that push the limit of what can be done without JavaScript. Benji TaylorFamily, Mercury. Motion and detail in consumer products, reference for the Family Drawer.

An intentionally short list. Better to know ten people's work deeply than to follow a hundred.

  • Devouring DetailsMariana Castilho. Interaction design and the details that make products feel alive.
